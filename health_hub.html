Restaurant Ordering Guide
🌯
Chipotle
Daily go-to · High protein options
▾
⭐ Best Order
Salad base · Chicken or Barbacoa · Black beans · Fajita veggies · Fresh salsa · Cheese · Lime
Removes ~200 cal by eliminating rice while keeping you full. ~40–50g protein. Ask for extra fajita veggies — they're free.
✅ Good Order
Brown rice (not white) · Barbacoa · Black beans · Fajita veggies · Fresh salsa · Cheese · Lettuce
Brown rice adds fiber, slows glucose absorption. Skip sour cream (150 cal). Skip corn salsa (higher sugar).
→ Current Order (Minor Upgrade)
White rice · Chicken · Cheese · Lettuce → Add: black beans + fajita veggies
Easiest first step — just add beans and veggies to what you already order. No other changes needed.
🐄
Chick-fil-A
Family & group lunches
▾
⭐ Best Order
Grilled Chicken Sandwich + Fruit Cup or Side Salad (light Italian)
320 cal, 28g protein. Swapping fries (400 cal) for fruit cup (70 cal) saves 330 calories in one choice.
⭐ Group Setting
12-count Grilled Nuggets + Side Salad + Water
290 cal, 38g protein — one of the best protein-to-calorie ratios of any fast food item.
→ If You Want Classic
Classic Sandwich (fried) + no sauce + water (not lemonade)
Skip the sauce and sugary drink. Keeps it around 450–500 cal. Acceptable occasionally.
🌮
Mexican / Tacos
Tacos · Enchiladas · Quesadillas
▾
⭐ Tacos
Beef or chicken · Corn tortillas (not flour) · Cheese · Lettuce · Pico de gallo
Corn tortillas: ~30% fewer calories + more fiber than flour. Pico = near-zero calories with lycopene benefits.
✅ Enchiladas
Beef or chicken · Red sauce (not cream sauce) · Sub: black beans instead of rice on the side
Red sauce = far fewer calories than cream or queso sauces. Black beans add fiber and protein.
→ Chips & Queso
Set a portion: 10–15 chips max · Salsa as primary dip · Queso as accent only
Salsa is nearly calorie-free. Use it as the main vehicle; queso becomes the flavor accent.
🍔
Burgers
Best build strategy
▾
⭐ Best Build
Single patty · Cheese · Lettuce · Mustard (not mayo) · No ketchup · Side salad instead of fries when available
Single vs double + mustard vs mayo + salad vs fries = difference between ~500 and ~1,100 calories. Same burger enjoyment.
🏠
Home Dinner Upgrades
Small swaps, big impact
▾
Tacos
90% lean ground beef · Corn tortillas · Pico instead of sour cream · Black beans on the side
Leaner beef + corn tortillas dramatically reduces saturated fat without changing the taste experience.
Chicken & Rice
Grilled/air-fryer chicken · Brown rice (swap white) · Air fryer roasted veggies
Brown rice is the easiest single swap your wife can make. Family won't notice within 2–3 weeks.
Pasta
Whole wheat pasta · Marinara sauce (not cream on weeknights) · Add spinach into the sauce
Whole wheat has 3x the fiber. Spinach wilts invisibly into marinara. Nobody knows it's there.
Baked Potato
Greek yogurt instead of sour cream · Same texture, 3x the protein
Genuinely indistinguishable on a hot potato. One of the easiest highest-impact daily swaps available.
